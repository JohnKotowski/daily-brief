AI Industry & Meta

Satya Nadella says AI models need an "emergency brake" — separate model from harness, log every meaningful action tamper-proof, and guarantee a human can pause or shut a model down mid-task TechCrunch "Assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start." Days after Anthropic's lost-control disclosures, Microsoft's CEO makes containment the safety story — and the product wedge.

In a Saturday X post Nadella argued it is time to "step back and assess the trust architecture" of AI: keep the model and the orchestration harness separate, externalise the controls, record every consequential action with human-readable, tamper-proof evidence, and make sure an authorised human can always stop a model in the middle of a task. The framing matters because it moves the industry's safety conversation from "make the model behave" to "assume it won't and build the cage" — which happens to be exactly what Microsoft is shipping (see MXC above). Expect enterprise buyers to start asking vendors where the brake is.

Innovation & Infra

Meta is building hardware-offloaded Compressed RAM ("CRAM") for Linux — near-DRAM read speed, writes far faster than ZRAM and Zswap, exposed as a private NUMA node Slashdot · via Phoronix / Tom's Hardware A direct answer to the AI-driven memory shortage: when you can't buy enough DRAM, compress what you have without giving up byte-level access.

At Linux Plumbers in Prague, Meta's Gregory Price presented CRAM: memory compression offloaded to hardware that still allows cacheline and byte access, surfaced to the kernel as a private NUMA node. Read-only data runs at raw DRAM speed and writes are much faster than the existing ZRAM/Zswap/swap paths. With DRAM prices spiking on AI build-outs, the hyperscalers are now engineering around the supply chain rather than waiting on it — and Meta says most of the pieces are already in mainline, so this is closer than a research talk.

Every · Mike Taylor: "You Already Signed This Off (In the Simulation)" — he cloned his manager, a GitHub exec and his CEO from their Slack history to pre-approve his work, and found the clones knew every rule but never when to break one Every · Oct 9 In an AI-abundant workplace the scarce resource is judgment-bearing sign-off time; persona files scale it only up to the boundary of taste.
