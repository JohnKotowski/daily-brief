Tailcat: netcat, but over Tailscale's data plane GitHub 569 points. A tiny tool that quietly removes a whole category of "how do I get this file between these two machines" problems.

Tailscale released Tailcat — netcat semantics running directly over the Tailscale data plane, so you get authenticated, encrypted, NAT-traversing pipes between any two nodes on your tailnet without opening a port or standing up a relay.

Practical for exactly the kind of thing you run: moving data between the Mac, containers, and a VPS without thinking about firewalls.
