A new form of ice, made at 2,357 °C Slashdot · ScienceA CEA team squeezed water to 2.3 million atmospheres and found a high-pressure ice phase that exists hotter than lava.

Under enough pressure water freezes no matter how hot it is; the new phase appeared at roughly 2,630 K and 2.3 million atmospheres, conditions found inside Neptune and Uranus and in the mantles of water-rich exoplanets. The reason it matters beyond the curiosity: ice-giant interiors are the biggest unknown in how those planets generate magnetic fields, and superionic and high-pressure ices are the leading explanation. One for the kids' science-fact rotation — "ice hotter than lava" lands well.
