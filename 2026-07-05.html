Armin Ronacher documents a counterintuitive regression: Anthropic's newest models (Opus 4.8, Sonnet 5) reliably hallucinate extra keys in nested tool-call JSON, breaking schemas that older models handled cleanly. His hypothesis is that post-training over-optimized against Claude Code's own flat, forgiving tool harness, so the models learned that specific shape rather than generalizing. It's a sharp reminder that tool schemas aren't neutral — models carry strong priors from their training environment and stumble on anything out-of-distribution. The practical fix is grammar-constrained decoding, but the deeper lesson matters for every agent builder: SOTA on benchmarks doesn't guarantee SOTA on your integration.
