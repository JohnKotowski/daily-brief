Constraint Decay: the fragility of LLM agents in backend code generation arXiv · HN 232 Paper measuring how quickly LLM agents lose track of constraints (auth scopes, schema invariants, deployment guards) as agent runs lengthen.

New paper from a team measuring "constraint decay" — the rate at which LLM coding agents quietly violate constraints (auth scopes, schema invariants, deployment guardrails) that were specified up front, as runs get longer. The result: even with frontier models, by the 30th turn most agents are operating against a degraded summary of the original spec, and the constraint-violation rate climbs predictably. The practical takeaway for anyone building agentic workflows: re-injecting constraints into every step beats relying on the model to remember them. This is a small paper that maps cleanly onto what most of us are seeing empirically.
