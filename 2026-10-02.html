Two years ago "vector database" was its own funding category; now the vendor with the best-known customers says the vector index should be one index among several in a general search engine. The reason is boring and convincing: real workloads mix "semantically similar" with "where customer = X and date > Y, grouped by plan," and a system built around approximate-nearest-neighbour cannot do the second part well.
