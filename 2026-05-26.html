Lenny Rachitsky: why SaaS freemium playbooks don't work in AI Lenny's Newsletter Free users in AI cost real GPU dollars — that breaks the funnel math that made SaaS work.

The core insight is unit economics: a SaaS freemium user costs pennies; a freemium AI user costs whatever the inference burns, which can be dollars per session. That inverts the classic playbook where you tolerate massive free funnels to win the 2% conversion — in AI, the free funnel can bankrupt you before the conversion lands. The strategic move is gated access, usage caps tied to identity, and pricing that reflects marginal cost from day one. Anyone shipping an AI product on a SaaS pricing template should read this before their next board meeting — and the lesson generalises to any product layered on a real per-call cost.
