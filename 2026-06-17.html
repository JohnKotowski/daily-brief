The rule binds government agencies and critical-infrastructure operators, effectively mandating post-quantum cryptography across regulated systems. The driver is "harvest now, decrypt later" — adversaries hoovering up encrypted traffic today to crack once quantum machines mature. It's one of the first concrete regulatory deadlines turning PQC from a roadmap slide into a procurement requirement, and a signal that vendors selling into European public sector need a migration plan now, not at the end of the decade.
