Google Research's "sleep" framework: models consolidate what they saw in context into their weights during offline phases Hacker News → Google ResearchWake phase uses context as short-term memory; sleep phase folds it into parameters. Retention reported from up to 10M tokens of context. 220 points.

Behrouz and Mirrokni's framework alternates a wake phase, where the model works normally and treats its context window as short-term memory, with a sleep phase that consolidates that context into the parameters, with the stated goal of continual learning without catastrophic forgetting. The post reports retention of material from contexts up to 10 million tokens and improved reasoning after consolidation, which, if it holds outside the lab, is the memory story every agent product has been faking with retrieval. Worth reading as a direction rather than a product; nothing here ships in Gemini yet.
