Five small Qwen models beat Fable 5 on hard coding problems with a $0 training trick Hacker News → GitHubA training-free harness has five copies of a 27B open model split a problem through a shared filesystem.

GVS5H ("Gorilla vs. 5 Humans") spins up five fresh Qwen3.8-27B instances that decompose a coding problem and coordinate through a shared plan, notes and solution on disk. On the 100 newest hard LiveCodeBench problems the orchestrated Qwen3.8 Flash Next scores 93.0% pass@1 against Fable 5's 90.4% at roughly 9% of the cost, and a locally served Qwen3.8-27B jumps from 66.8% to 92.4%. The authors caveat that the gains are not universal and the frontier comparison used a single-pass, older scaffold. Relevant to the Cobra POC: orchestration of cheap models is now a real design option, not a research curiosity.

Goose: a memory-safe systems language designed by a human, implemented almost entirely by Claude Hacker NewsWouter van Oortmerssen (Lobster, FlatBuffers) wrote the spec; Claude Fable wrote the ~30k-line compiler.

Goose has C- and Rust-like syntax but no heap allocator, garbage collector or lifetime annotations: dynamic values live inline on compiler-managed data stacks, references stay valid as arrays grow, and threads share no memory. The repo ships a whole-program compiler, spec, standard library, samples and CI across Windows, macOS and Linux. It is the cleanest data point so far for "human designs the language, AI implements it" as a working method, and the 374-point HN thread is mostly engineers arguing about whether that counts as authorship.

Node2Shell becomes HTTP2Shell: critical Brotli bug (CVSS 9.2) reaches Python requests/httpx, Envoy, Istio Hacker News → AikidoThe blast radius widened from Node.js to most HTTP stacks that decode br.

CVE-2026-111497 is an out-of-bounds write in Google's Brotli decoder (versions 1.0.3 to 1.2.0, brotlicffi 1.2.0.x) that a remote unauthenticated attacker can use to crash, leak or potentially execute code. Python clients such as requests and httpx auto-load Brotli if it is installed, so they are exposed alongside Node, Bun, Envoy and Istio. Fix: Brotli 1.2.1 or brotlicffi 1.2.1.0 plus patched runtimes, then rebuild containers; the stopgap is dropping br from Accept-Encoding and rejecting br responses. Worth a pip list | grep -i brotli across the PricingSaaS and PuckFinder services on Monday.
