Study of 159,041 Lovable projects finds many apps exposing personal data without a login Crissy Field7,403 sites had email tables readable without logging in. Relevant to anything you have built on Lovable and Supabase.

Researchers working with Handelsblatt analysed 159,041 Lovable projects found in Common Crawl, 34,545 of which used Supabase. Of the 24,664 sites that sent database records on page load, 7,403 (30%) had tables containing email addresses that could be read without logging in; the authors say this is not a confirmed leak rate. One German lifestyle forum exposed more than 80,000 profiles and more than 200,000 chat messages.

Google Project Zero drops its 30-day patch grace period and will cut disclosure deadlines to 30 days by 2028 Project ZeroLLMs let attackers reverse-engineer patches almost immediately, so the grace period goes.

Project Zero is replacing its "90 + 30" policy with "Full 90": bugs are disclosed 90 days after being reported to the vendor, with no extra rollout time. The deadline drops to 60 days on February 2, 2027 and 30 days on January 3, 2028. The team says LLMs let attackers find bugs and reverse-engineer patches almost immediately, and that it now finds several vulnerabilities a week in targets where it once found a few a year.
