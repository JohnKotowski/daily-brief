The Classification of Finite Simple Groups is now fully formalized in Lean, and the check found errata in the published proof Hacker News → Axiom MathThousands of pages verified in Lean's kernel; one published closure assertion turned out to have counterexamples.

Axiom Math ran every step of the first-generation CFSG proof, one of the largest results in mathematics, through Lean's kernel. Along the way they found counterexamples to a closure assertion in a published route to the Unbalanced Group Theorem and published an alternative argument that closes the gap. Two things make this an AI story rather than a pure maths one: the formalization was AI-assisted at scale, and it is the clearest demonstration yet that machine-checked proofs catch errors that decades of human refereeing did not. Expect this to show up in every "AI for science" deck for a year. 207 points on HN, with the mathematicians in the thread mostly impressed and the Lean people mostly tired.

Python's experimental JIT is being removed from CPython Hacker News → LWNPEP 836 withdrawn; the 3.13 JIT goes away over maintenance cost, compatibility and no clear win.

After the steering council paused JIT work in June pending an accepted PEP, the maintainers withdrew PEP 836 and the copy-and-patch JIT added in 3.13 is on its way out. LWN's account is the full one: the concerns were maintenance burden on a small team, compatibility with the free-threaded build, and the lack of agreed success metrics after two releases of modest speedups. The faster-CPython effort continues through the specializing interpreter, which is where most of the real gains came from anyway. If you benchmark Python, nothing changes for you; if you were waiting for "Python gets a JIT," stop waiting. 498 points on HN.
