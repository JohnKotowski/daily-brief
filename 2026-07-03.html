Virginia becomes the first state to ban the sale of geolocation data HN 745 An outright ban — not a consent regime — on selling consumer location data. A major escalation in US state privacy law.

Virginia has banned the sale of consumer geolocation data outright — not opt-out, not consent-gated, banned.
