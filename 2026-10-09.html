Cactus Compute shipped Whistle as a single 16.9 MB file: an open speech-recognition model that runs CPU-only, covers seven languages, and starts emitting tokens 11 ms after audio arrives. Innovation & Infra

Margaret Hamilton, who led the Apollo flight-software team at MIT and gave "software engineering" its name, has died at 90 MIT News · Slashdot Her priority-scheduling code is why the Apollo 11 computer shed low-priority tasks instead of crashing during the 1202 alarms three minutes before touchdown.

Hamilton joined MIT's Instrumentation Laboratory in 1959 and ran the on-board flight software effort for Apollo through the mid-1970s, with more than 130 publications over her career before founding Higher Order Software and later Hamilton Technologies. She insisted on treating software as an engineering discipline with error handling and asynchronous priority scheduling at a time when it was budgeted as an afterthought; the famous photo of her beside the printed stack of Apollo code is the one every systems class shows. She received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016.

The first nuclear clocks are ticking: teams in Vienna and Beijing independently locked a clock to a thorium-229 nuclear transition instead of an electron shell Nature · Slashdot Nuclear transitions are far less sensitive to stray fields than atomic ones, which is the path to ultra-precise clocks that can leave the lab.

Published in Nature as "A nuclear clock synchronized to 229Th": both groups reference a laser to the uniquely low-energy thorium-229 nuclear transition and already report stability on the order of a second lost over millions of years, with a clear path to beating the best optical atomic clocks. The practical draw is portability and robustness — nuclear clocks shrug off electromagnetic noise that forces today's optical lattice clocks into shielded rooms. Downstream uses are GPS without satellites, dark-matter searches and tests of whether physical constants drift. The NYT story is paywalled; the Nature paper and Slashdot's summary are linked.
