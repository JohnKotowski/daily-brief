Australia will legally require big AI data centers to be net power generators pv magazine Albanese's plan: large data centers must put at least as much clean energy into the grid as they draw, and cover their own connection + water costs.

Australia announced it will make it a legal obligation for the next generation of large-scale data centers to underwrite new power supply — putting at least as much energy into the grid as they take out, paying their full share of grid-connection costs so households don't, and minimizing water use. It converts a set of March 2026 "expectations" into a binding framework expected to be legislated in early 2027, overseen by a new Office of AI. This is one of the first hard regulatory answers to the AI-buildout energy problem: rather than banning or rationing data centers, make them additive to the grid. Expect other governments staring down the same demand curve to study it closely.
